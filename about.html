ABOUT US

A little more about Serein.

Serein started as a simple idea: taking care of your mind shouldn't feel complicated, clinical, or like one more thing to figure out on your own.

OUR STORY

Why we started Serein.

We noticed that a lot of people put off asking for support, not because they didn't need it, but because the first step felt too big. Booking a session, explaining yourself to a stranger, or admitting things weren't okay all felt like hurdles on their own.

Serein was built to lower that first step. Whether that's writing a few lines in a journal, joining a group session, or simply reading something that makes you feel less alone, we wanted a space that meets people where they are.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

How we approach care.

We're not here to replace therapy or medical care, and we're upfront about that. We see Serein as a companion to professional support: a gentle place to start, reflect, and keep going in between.

Every part of Serein, from the tone of our writing to how our sessions are run, is built around one question: does this make it easier for someone to take care of themselves?

OUR VALUES

What we hold onto.

A few principles that guide how we build and how we show up for the people who use Serein.

Compassion first

We lead with kindness, not judgement, in every part of the experience.

Privacy & safety

What you share stays yours. We built Serein to feel like a safe space, always.

No pressure

There's no right way to use Serein. Take what helps, skip what doesn't.

Accessible support

We keep our options simple and affordable, so cost isn't another barrier.
